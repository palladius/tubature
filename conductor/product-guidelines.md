# Product Guidelines

## Voice & Tone
- **Fun, colorful, kid-friendly**: Joyful, encouraging, and welcoming to young players (ages 6–8).
- **Minimal Text**: Avoid text-heavy tutorials or dialogs; rely on visual affordances and animations.

## User Experience (UX)
- **Large Tap Targets**: All interactive elements must have tap targets of at least **56dp+** for comfortable interaction on touch screens.
- **Smooth Animations**: Responsive, snappy feedback (animations ≤ 200ms) for rotations and fluid transitions.
- **Clear Visual Feedback**: Instant visual response when tiles rotate, connect, or complete water circuits.

## Visual Design
- **Bold Rounded Pipes**: Clear, chunky, colorful pipe designs that are easy to distinguish.
- **Warm Backgrounds**: Friendly color palettes suited for kids.
- **Creature Mascots**: Dragon 🐉, Wizard 🧙, and Space 🚀 themes giving personality to each puzzle set.

## Ethics & Philosophy
- **Pure Gameplay**: No monetization, no ads, no paywalls.
- **No Accounts**: Zero registration or account requirements; instant pick-up-and-play.
- **Safe & Delightful**: Safe environment for children to explore logic and spatial reasoning.
